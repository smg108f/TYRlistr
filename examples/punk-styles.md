# 🧷 Punk styles

The -punk family of speculative-fiction styles that grew out of cyberpunk. Synonyms fold together (sandalpunk takes in bronzepunk, atompunk takes in raypunk, aetherpunk takes in magitech and dungeonpunk); punk rock and its music subgenres are left out. Drafted by Claude Opus as an eighth worked example.
